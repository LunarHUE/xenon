<Section title="When to use">

<Definitions>
  <Definition term="Button">Runs an action, like saving a form or opening a dialog.</Definition>
  <Definition term="Toggle">Switches something on or off and exposes `aria-pressed`.</Definition>
  <Definition term="Link">
    Goes to another page. Use an `a`, or Button with `as="a"` when the link needs a pending or
    disabled state.
  </Definition>
</Definitions>
